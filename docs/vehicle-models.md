# Vehicle model sourcing (Step 5 shortlist)

Nothing here is downloaded until it's approved. Downloaded files go to `assets-src/` (gitignored) and are converted by `scripts/build-vehicles.mjs` into `public/models/vehicles/`.

| Vehicle | Proposed source | License | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Buggy | [Kenney Car Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit), `kenney_car-kit.zip` | CC0 | 3.4 MB | 40+ cars, trucks, vans and kart racers in glTF; use the best off-road body. Roll cage added procedurally if needed. |
| Volt Roadster | Same Kenney Car Kit (sports/race body) | CC0 | (same file) | Original design: procedural thruster pods and a wheel tuck on a generic sports body, no branding. |
| Bicycle / MTB | [Quaternius LowPoly Public Transport](https://opengameart.org/content/lowpoly-public-transport), `Public Transport.zip` | CC0 | 1.4 MB | Includes a bicycle (Blend/OBJ/FBX). Used only if wheels and bars can be split; otherwise a detailed procedural rebuild. |
| Hover car | [Quaternius Ultimate Spaceships](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatespaceships.html) | CC0 | not listed | glTF, 10 small craft × 5 colours. The site has no direct link, so it may need a manual download. |
| Motorbike | No CC0 model found | — | — | Options: CC-BY models from Poly Pizza (free with a credit line), or a detailed procedural rebuild. |
| Jet wing, snowboard, skis | No CC0 models found | — | — | Detailed procedural rebuilds (simple shapes, easy to do well). |
| Wingsuit | Procedural | — | — | Cloth membrane between the rider's arms and legs; works with every outfit. |

Sources checked: Kenney, Quaternius, OpenGameArt and Poly Pizza (most Poly Pizza bikes are "Poly by Google", CC-BY).
