# Model credits

- `riders.glb`: built from **Ultimate Modular Men** by Quaternius, released under CC0 1.0 (public domain, no attribution required). Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularcharacters.html
  Rebuilt by `scripts/build-riders.mjs`: all 11 characters merged onto one shared skeleton, animations stripped, normals/weights quantized (KHR_mesh_quantization).

- `nature.glb`: built from the **Stylized Nature MegaKit** (Standard, free) by Quaternius, released under CC0 1.0. Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/stylizednaturemegakit.html
  Rebuilt by `scripts/build-nature.mjs`: 12 picked models (pines, broadleaf trees, a bush, rocks, grass, flowers); foliage keeps its cut-out textures, bark/rock textures replaced by flat colours, normals and vertex colours quantized.

Everything else in the game (terrain, vehicles, structures, sky, audio) is procedural.
